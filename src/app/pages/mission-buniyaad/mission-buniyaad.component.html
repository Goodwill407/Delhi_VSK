Mission Buniyad is a comprehensive initiative aimed at transforming the educational landscape in the capital city of India. Rooted in the vision of fostering holistic development and ensuring quality education for all, Mission Buniyad endeavors to address the foundational learning needs of students across various socio-economic strata.

At its core, Mission Buniyad is committed to enhancing the basic literacy and numeracy skills of children, particularly in government schools and underserved communities. Through a multifaceted approach, the mission seeks to identify and rectify gaps in foundational learning, laying a strong educational groundwork for students to thrive academically and beyond


Key components of Mission Buniyad include:

1. Curriculum Enhancement:

The mission aims to revamp the curriculum to prioritize foundational skills in literacy and numeracy, ensuring that students receive a strong educational foundation from the outset.

2. Teacher Training and Capacity Building:

Recognizing the pivotal role of educators, Mission Buniyad invests in comprehensive training programs to equip teachers with innovative pedagogical techniques and strategies tailored to improve foundational learning outcomes.

3. Assessment and Monitoring:

Rigorous assessment mechanisms are put in place to gauge student progress and identify areas of improvement. Continuous monitoring and evaluation help track the effectiveness of interventions and inform data-driven decision-making.

4. Community Engagement:

Mission Buniyad fosters partnerships with local communities, parents, and stakeholders to garner support and participation in educational initiatives. Community involvement is pivotal in ensuring the sustained success and relevance of the mission.

5. Technology Integration:

Leveraging the power of technology, the mission integrates digital tools and resources to enhance teaching and learning experiences, making education more accessible and engaging for students.

6. Inclusive Approach:

With a commitment to inclusivity, Mission Buniyad prioritizes reaching marginalized and vulnerable populations, ensuring that every child, regardless of background, has access to quality education and equal opportunities for growth and development.

By addressing the foundational learning needs of students and fostering a culture of excellence in education, Mission Buniyad aspires to pave the way for a brighter future, where every child in can realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.
