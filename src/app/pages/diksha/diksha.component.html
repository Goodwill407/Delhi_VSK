At Diksha, we strive to revolutionize learning by fostering a dynamic educational environment that empowers students for success in the 21st century. Our vision is to redefine education delivery through technology and innovation,

transcending traditional methods.

We provide a comprehensive learning platform accessible to students and educators nationwide, supporting proficiency in 36 Indian languages. Our key features include:

- E-Textbooks
- Explanation Resources
- Practice Resources
- Learning Resources
- Teacher-Centric Resources

Notable offerings include:

- Energized Textbooks (ETB): Transitioning from physical to 'phygital' textbooks.

- PM eVidya: Unifying digital, online, and broadcast education efforts for inclusive access.

- Digital Teacher Training: A nationwide initiative enhancing the quality of school education through integrated training.

- Content Sourcing: Curating high-quality content in a controlled manner.


Diksha serves as a customizable National Digital Infrastructure for states to implement teacher-centric initiatives. Educators can utilize Diksha to access and create top-tier teaching, learning, and assessment resources

across all subjects and levels in various Indian languages. Our mission is to bolster the capabilities of our teachers, recognizing them as the backbone of our educational system.
