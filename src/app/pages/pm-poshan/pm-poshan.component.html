The Pradhan Mantri Poshan Abhiyan (PM Poshan) is a flagship initiative launched by the Government of India aimed at addressing malnutrition among children and pregnant women. Launched in 2018, the scheme focuses on the holistic development and nourishment of children under the age of 6 years and pregnant women and lactating mothers. Poshan Abhiyan is part of the overarching National Nutrition Mission (NNM) aimed at achieving a malnutrition-free India by 2022.

Here are some key features and components of the PM Poshan scheme:

1. Nutrition Awareness: One of the primary objectives of PM Poshan is to create awareness about the importance of proper nutrition and healthcare during the crucial early years of a child's development. This includes educating parents, caregivers, and communities about the significance of breastfeeding, proper infant and young child feeding practices, hygiene, and sanitation.

2. Service Delivery: The scheme emphasizes improving the delivery of essential services related to healthcare and nutrition. This includes access to antenatal care, immunization, and healthcare services for pregnant women, lactating mothers, and children. It also focuses on ensuring the availability of nutritious food and supplements, such as iron and folic acid tablets, to pregnant and lactating women.

3. Growth Monitoring: PM Poshan emphasizes regular growth monitoring of children to track their nutritional status and identify any growth faltering or malnutrition at an early stage. This enables timely intervention and support to ensure healthy growth and development.

4. Supplementary Nutrition: The scheme provides supplementary nutrition to children under the age of 6 years through the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) centers. This includes take-home rations, hot-cooked meals, and nutritious snacks to bridge the nutritional gap and promote healthy growth and development.

5. Convergence: PM Poshan promotes convergence among various departments and stakeholders involved in nutrition-related programs and initiatives. It encourages coordination and collaboration between health, education, and other relevant sectors to achieve maximum impact and effectiveness in addressing malnutrition.

6. Behaviour Change Communication (BCC): Behaviour Change Communication is an integral component of the PM Poshan scheme. It involves using various communication channels, including mass media, community-based activities, and interpersonal communication, to promote positive nutrition and health-seeking behaviors among families and communities.

7. Capacity Building: The scheme focuses on capacity building at the grassroots level, including training of frontline health workers, anganwadi workers, and community volunteers. This equips them with the necessary knowledge and skills to effectively implement nutrition interventions and provide appropriate counseling and support to families.
